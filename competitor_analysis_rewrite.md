## 2. Competitor Analysis

Sea Horse, IKEA, and MUJI are the three most accessible pillow brands in Hong Kong. This section compares them across seven dimensions — competition scope, positioning, product, price, packaging, distribution, promotion, and reliability — to identify where Sea Horse holds an advantage and where it is exposed to competitive pressure.

> [!TIP]
> **開場白的寫法要點**：原文只說「these three brands have their strengths and weaknesses in terms of SWOT analysis」——這太空泛了。改寫後的開場直接告訴讀者：(1) 比較誰、(2) 比較什麼、(3) 比較的目的。一句話設定整個章節的期望。

---

### 2.1 Competition Scope

**Direct competition.** IKEA and MUJI are Sea Horse's primary direct competitors. All three sell pillows in Hong Kong, target customers who seek a better sleep experience, and compete on price, product range, and store accessibility.

**Indirect competition.** The core need behind a pillow purchase is improved sleep quality. Products such as weighted blankets, sleep masks, white noise machines, and aromatherapy diffusers address the same need through different means. Brands like Rituals — and MUJI's own sleep-wellness range — fall into this category. This broadens the competitive field beyond pillow-to-pillow comparison: Sea Horse must defend its share of the customer's total "sleep budget," not just the pillow budget.

> [!TIP]
> **改動說明**：
> - 刪掉了定義句（「Direct competitors are brands that…」）。你的讀者是教授，不需要課本定義。
> - 最後一句把 indirect competition 的影響說得更具體：不是一般化地說「this is a weakness」，而是點出「sleep budget」這個概念。

### 2.2 Target Market and Positioning

Each brand occupies a distinct position in the Hong Kong pillow market:

| Brand | Primary Appeal | Core Message |
|---|---|---|
| Sea Horse | Neck support, ergonomic design, local brand trust | "38 years of professional sleep authority" |
| IKEA | Wide choice, low entry price, shopping convenience | "Accessible, functional design for everyone" |
| MUJI | Simple design, easy care, lifestyle consistency | "No-brand quality goods — enough is enough" |

Sea Horse positions itself as a **specialist**: its product pages emphasise cervical support, ergonomic posture, and decades of local credibility. IKEA positions itself as a **generalist**: it offers the widest range of materials and price points, backed by member rewards and a service-led shopping experience. MUJI positions itself as a **curator**: it proposes a whole lifestyle built on material selection, process streamlining, and package simplification (MUJI, n.d.).

These positioning differences shape every subsequent comparison in this chapter.

### 2.3 Product Strategy and Differentiation

> [!TIP]
> **合併原因**：原文的 §2.3 Product Strategy 和 §2.5 Product Features 講的是同一件事。合併後，先講產品種類，再講材質，最後講功能差異。

**Product range.** Sea Horse focuses on pillow shape and firmness. Its product line includes flat, cervical (neck-support), and wave pillows, each available in different heights and firmness levels (Seahorse, n.d.-b, n.d.-c). IKEA offers the broadest selection by filling type — polyester fibre, down and feather, memory foam, and latex — as well as ergonomic designs such as the dual-layer ROSENSKÄRM and the cooling-side KVARNVEN (IKEA Hong Kong and Macau, n.d.-b, n.d.-e, n.d.-g). MUJI takes a simpler approach. Its pillows use breathable pipe filling, are machine-washable, and include functional variants such as a quick-dry pillow, an anti-mite pillow, and a height-adjustable pipe pillow (MUJI Hong Kong, n.d.-a to n.d.-f).

**Comparative summary:**

| Dimension | Sea Horse | IKEA | MUJI |
|---|---|---|---|
| Design focus | Neck support, shape variety | Material variety, ergonomic layers | Simplicity, washability |
| Key materials | High-density PU foam, memory foam | Polyester, down, memory foam, latex | Fibre-tube pipe filling |
| Unique selling point | Cervical-support specialisation | Widest filling-type range | Machine-washable, easy care |

Sea Horse's depth in neck-support design is a clear strength for customers with cervical needs. However, its narrow range of filling materials limits its appeal to customers who prioritise breathability, natural fill, or cooling features — segments where IKEA is stronger.

### 2.4 Price Competitiveness

Price is often the first filter for pillow shoppers. The three brands occupy different price bands:

| Brand | Entry Price | Upper Range | Price Positioning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sea Horse | HK$188 | HK$540 | Mid-range, no budget option |
| IKEA | HK$59.9 | HK$549.9 `[需確認上限]` | Widest spread, lowest entry |
| MUJI | HK$78 | HK$300 `[需確認上限]` | Moderate, compact range |

IKEA's entry price of HK$59.9 is less than one-third of Sea Horse's HK$188. This gives IKEA a decisive **cost-competitive advantage** at the lower end: price-sensitive shoppers can try an IKEA pillow with minimal commitment. MUJI's HK$78 entry point also undercuts Sea Horse. Sea Horse's highest entry price creates a barrier that **excludes the budget segment entirely** — a positioning trade-off that reinforces its specialist image but limits its addressable market.

> [!TIP]
> **改動說明**：
> - 原文最後幾句非常重複（「IKEA has the lowest entry price and the broadest visible range. MUJI's entry price of HK$78 is also lower than Sea Horse's. Sea Horse's price position is not competitive at the lower end...」）。改寫後一段話講完。
> - 最後一句不只說「weakness」，而是把它框架為「positioning trade-off」——這是更高級的 Marketing 分析語言。

### 2.5 Packaging and Logistics

**Environmental positioning.** IKEA and MUJI both use recyclable packaging materials. IKEA's packaging meets EU safety standards and is non-toxic; MUJI uses plastics free from harmful chemicals. Sea Horse uses non-degradable clear plastic bags. In an era of rising environmental awareness, this puts Sea Horse at a **green-marketing disadvantage**.

**Logistics efficiency.** IKEA compresses its pillows into compact packages, reducing per-unit shipping volume and lowering transport costs — a structural advantage that supports its low pricing. Sea Horse and MUJI ship pillows uncompressed, which increases per-unit logistics cost.

**Visual appeal.** MUJI's plain white packaging with minimal text reinforces its "no-brand" identity. IKEA's packaging is functional and recognisable. Sea Horse's clear-bag packaging is practical but visually undifferentiated — it neither signals premium quality nor communicates brand identity at shelf level.

> [!TIP]
> **改動說明**：
> - 原文把三個不同的議題（環保、物流、視覺）混在一起寫。改寫後用三個小標題分開，每個只講一件事。
> - 刪掉了「Packaging is used to protect products and attract consumers」這種教科書句子。

### 2.6 Distribution and Post-Purchase Support

> [!TIP]
> **合併原因**：原文的 §2.3 Place Utility 和 §2.7 Perceived Risk Reduction 有大量重複（退貨政策、試用期等）。合併後，「買到手」和「買了之後」的體驗放在同一節分析。

#### 2.6.1 Physical and Online Presence

| Channel | Sea Horse | IKEA | MUJI |
|---|---|---|---|
| Physical stores | **20+ stores** across HK Island, Kowloon, and NT | 4 stores (Causeway Bay, Kowloon Bay, Shatin, Tsuen Wan) | ~21 stores territory-wide |
| Online store | Own website + HKTVmall | Official e-commerce site | Official online shop |
| Pickup options | N/A | In-store, pickup points, pickup lockers, supermarket pickup | Standard delivery, self-pickup |

> [!WARNING]
> 原文寫 Sea Horse「only 4 physical stores」是 **錯誤** 的。根據 HSBC 商戶優惠頁面及 Sea Horse 官網，Sea Horse 目前在全港有 20 多間門市，地點包括銅鑼灣、旺角、深水埗、藍田、將軍澳、馬鞍山、沙田、屯門、天水圍、荃灣等。修正後的論點完全不同——Sea Horse 的門市密度與 MUJI 相當，遠超 IKEA。

With 20-plus stores, Sea Horse matches MUJI in physical footprint and far exceeds IKEA's four locations. However, IKEA compensates through a **multi-format pickup network** — pick-up points, lockers, and supermarket collection — that extends its reach beyond its four stores. IKEA's pickup fee is HK$28, waived for orders above HK$150, and most orders are ready within one to five working days (IKEA Hong Kong and Macau, n.d.-a, n.d.-d).

MUJI's delivery policy: `[需確認: 原文寫「below HK$600 costs HK$60」，但查證顯示 MUJI 的政策是淨消費低於 HK$3,000 收 HK$300。請到 muji.com.hk 核實最新運費。]`

#### 2.6.2 Return Policy and Risk Reduction

Pillows are experience goods — customers cannot fully evaluate comfort before extended use. Post-purchase policies therefore play a critical role in reducing perceived risk.

| Policy | Sea Horse | IKEA | MUJI |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trial period | 60-day trial (selected memory pillows) | **100-day trial** (ergonomic pillows) | No extended trial |
| Return policy | No return after sales slip issued (neck pillows) | **365-day return** (new, unused) | 14-day return with receipt |
| Risk-reduction approach | Product-led (support guarantee) | Policy-led (generous trial + return) | Maintenance-led (washable, anti-mite) |

IKEA offers the strongest risk-reduction package: a 100-day pillow trial combined with a 365-day general return policy. Sea Horse's 60-day trial on selected items is respectable, but the no-return clause on other products creates an **inconsistent experience** that may deter cautious buyers. MUJI does not highlight a long trial, but reduces practical ownership risk through machine-washable, quick-dry, and mite-resistant features.

> [!TIP]
> **改動說明**：
> - 加了「experience goods」這個 Marketing 術語來解釋為什麼 risk reduction 重要
> - 用「Product-led / Policy-led / Maintenance-led」三個標籤概括三種不同的風險降低策略——這是更有深度的分析
> - 不再重複 §2.6.1 已經講過的內容

### 2.7 Promotion and Brand Strategy

> [!TIP]
> **合併原因**：原文有兩個版本的 §2.4。這裡合併為一個章節，先講推廣工具，再講品牌信息。

#### Promotional Tools

| Tool | Sea Horse | IKEA | MUJI |
|---|---|---|---|
| Traditional media | Celebrity endorsement (Liza Wang 汪明荃, 35-year ambassador) | TV ads, in-store experiential displays | Minimal — rarely runs large campaigns |
| Digital channels | Facebook, HKTVmall, own website | Social media, IKEA Family app | Social media, MUJI app |
| Loyalty programme | None | **IKEA Family** (yuu rewards integration) | MUJI Passport |
| Experiential marketing | Limited | **Bedroom showrooms** (try before you buy) | Store atmosphere as brand experience |

**Sea Horse** builds its brand through **authority and heritage**. It has been Hong Kong's best-selling mattress and pillow brand for 35 consecutive years (1989–2023) and holds multiple industry awards, including the Hong Kong Distinguished Brand Leader Award (2021), the Hong Kong Premier Brand Award (2020), and the Reader's Digest Trusted Brand Platinum Award (2018) (Sea Horse, n.d.; Hong Kong Brand Development Council, n.d.). Its partnership with Liza Wang reinforces an image of trustworthiness and reliability (Sing Tao Daily, 2023). However, Sea Horse's promotional strategy relies heavily on past credentials and does not include a loyalty programme to drive repeat purchase.

**IKEA** uses a **service-and-engagement model**. In-store bedroom displays let customers test pillows physically — lowering the purchase barrier through experiential marketing. The IKEA Family programme, integrated with yuu rewards, creates a loyalty loop that encourages repeat visits (IKEA Hong Kong and Macau, n.d.-h). IKEA also runs creative brand campaigns; for example, the 2024 RESTEN campaign in Singapore reimagined the FRAKTA bag as a portable pillow under the tagline "Leave the rest to IKEA," linking the brand to the modern need for on-the-go rest (Dezeen, 2024).

**MUJI** follows a **philosophy-driven model**. Rather than selling individual products, MUJI proposes a lifestyle — "this is enough." Its three core principles — selection of materials, streamlining of processes, simplification of packages (MUJI, n.d.) — serve as both a production philosophy and a brand message. MUJI's promotion is the store experience itself: the calm layout, natural materials, and understated design attract customers who share these values.

> [!TIP]
> **改動說明**：
> - 用 authority-and-heritage / service-and-engagement / philosophy-driven 三個模型來概括三種推廣策略
> - RESTEN campaign 已標明是 Singapore 的活動
> - Sea Horse 各個獎項只提一次，不在多個段落重複
> - 不再有「其他版本」——品牌信息融入了推廣策略的描述中

## 改寫前後對比：寫作技巧總結

| 習慣 | ❌ 原文做法 | ✅ 改寫做法 |
|---|---|---|
| 結論句 | 反覆寫「This is a weakness for Sea Horse」（7+ 次） | 每節用不同的分析語言總結（competitive disadvantage / positioning trade-off / inconsistent experience 等） |
| 結構 | 先介紹概念定義，再逐個品牌描述 | 直接用比較框架，三個品牌並排分析 |
| 數據呈現 | 在段落中夾雜數字 | 用表格呈現數據，段落只做分析 |
| 品牌分析 | 描述性（「Sea Horse does X, IKEA does Y」） | 框架性（specialist vs generalist vs curator） |
| 章節職責 | 多個章節講同一件事（產品 × 2，退貨 × 2） | 每節只負責一個議題 |
| 語氣 | 像課本（「Direct competitors are brands that…」） | 像分析報告（直接進入比較） |
| 引用 | 獎項和數據在多處重複出現 | 每個數據點只出現一次，放在最相關的章節 |
