# Sea Horse vs. IKEA vs. MUJI: Hong Kong Pillow Market Competitor Analysis

A competitive marketing analysis of **Sea Horse** against **IKEA** and **MUJI** in Hong Kong's pillow market, covering positioning, product strategy, pricing, packaging/logistics, omnichannel distribution, post-purchase risk reduction, and Consumer Council reliability benchmarks.

## Contents
- `SeaHorse_Competitor_Analysis.pptx` — 8-slide competitive analysis deck comparing Sea Horse, IKEA, and MUJI
- `perceptual_map_hk_pillows_v5.png` — Price vs. In-store Experience perceptual map for Hong Kong pillow brands
- `competitor_analysis_review.md` — Fact-checking and structural audit log verifying 17 quantitative/factual claims against official sources
- `competitor_analysis_rewrite.md` — Restructured comparative draft incorporating verified corrections and consolidated sections
- `seahorse_marketing_research_zh.docx` — Supporting Chinese marketing audit and brand-repositioning research report
